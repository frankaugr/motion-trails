## A. Screen recording (item 1) — capture on a *physical* device

Apple requires a recording from a real device on the latest iOS (a Simulator recording won't be
accepted). Record your screen (Control Centre → Screen Recording), then:

1. **Launch the app** from the Home Screen (start the recording before tapping the icon).
2. On the first-run screen, tap **"Try a demo clip"** → it opens the editor with a built-in sample.
3. Tap **Generate** and let the trail effect render; show the result playing.
4. Tap **Save** → show the **Photos "Add" permission prompt** appear and accept it.
5. (Recommended, to show the other permission) Back in the library tap **Record a clip** → show the
   **Camera permission prompt**, record ~3 seconds, then Generate.
6. End on the finished video.

Keep it ~45–90s. This covers launch, the core flow, the result, and both permission prompts. Attach
it directly in your reply message to App Review.

## B. Paste into App Review Information → Notes

**App purpose & target audience (item 3)**
Motion Trails turns a short, fixed-camera (tripod or propped-up) video clip into a single video in
which fast-moving subjects leave long-exposure-style trails over an otherwise still scene — the
"daylight long exposure" look, produced by on-device computational video processing rather than a
slow camera shutter. It solves a problem that normally requires a dedicated camera, ND filters, a
tripod and desktop editing: Motion Trails produces the effect from an ordinary iPhone clip in a few
taps. Target audience: hobbyist and enthusiast photographers and videographers, birdwatchers and
wildlife fans, cyclists/runners/action-sports creators, and social-media creators who want a
distinctive motion effect. The app is a one-time paid purchase with every feature included —
there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

**How to set up and access the main features (item 4)**
No account, login, or credentials are required — the app works fully offline on first launch.
- Fastest path to the core feature (no footage needed): on the first-run screen tap **"Try a demo
  clip"**. This creates a sample project from a **built-in** clip and opens the editor. Tap
  **Generate** to render the trail effect. (A sample clip ships inside the app — no file to provide.)
- Use your own footage: tap **Record a clip** (grants Camera; capture a few seconds with the phone
  held still) or **Import a clip** (pick a video from Photos). Then adjust the controls and Generate.
- Export: after generating, tap **Save** to add the video to Photos, or **Share**.

**External services, tools, or platforms (item 5)**
None. All processing happens on-device using Apple frameworks only (AVFoundation, Core Image, Vision).
The app has no backend or server, makes no network requests, and uses no third-party SDKs, analytics,
advertising, authentication, payment processors, or external/cloud AI services. No data is collected
and user media never leaves the device.

**Regional differences (item 6)**
The app functions identically in all regions. There is no region-locked content, no
region-dependent features, and no server/region dependencies. (The interface is in English.)

**Regulated industry / protected third-party material (item 7)**
Not a regulated industry. The only third-party material included is one short bundled demo video clip,
offered as the optional "Try a demo clip" sample. It is from Pexels and used under the Pexels License,
which grants free commercial use and modification with no attribution required:

- License: https://www.pexels.com/license/
All other media in the app is created by the user or generated by the app's own rendering engine.
