## Description (≤4000) — ~2300/4000

Motion Trails turns a few seconds of locked-off video into a daylight long exposure: every fast-moving subject leaves a trail of silhouettes across a perfectly still scene. No ND filter, no slow shutter, no desktop editing — just your iPhone or iPad.

Prop your phone on a ledge or tripod, point it at something fast, and Motion Trails paints each pass — a bird, a cyclist, a wave, a passing car — as a streak of silhouettes over a clean, frozen background.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Record a few seconds with the camera held still, or import a clip you already have.
2. Tune the look on a live preview — density, speed threshold, fade and colour.
3. Export a clean, loop-ready video to Photos, or share it anywhere.

KEYED ON SPEED
Motion Trails detects how fast something moves, not just that something changed. Fast subjects leave trails; slow drift — clouds, swaying branches, shifting light — is ignored. Your trails stay crisp and your sky stays clean.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Subject contrast — dark silhouettes (birds on sky), bright shapes (white birds, headlights, splashes), or a different colour.
• Speed threshold — from only the fastest movers to slower action.
• Density — sparse and graphic, or a dense, continuous ribbon.
• Fade — let trails persist, or melt away over time.
• Colour — keep it natural, or sweep older trails through a colour gradient as they age.
• Frozen or live — hold the scene as a still, or let clouds and water keep moving under the trails.
• Handheld camera — add lifelike sway, with optional depth parallax so nearer trails move more than distant ones.
• Ignore regions — mask out busy water or foliage so trails only form where you want.
• Crop presets — 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9 for any feed.

KEEP YOUR WORK
Every clip becomes a project you can reopen, re-tune and re-export. Rename it, manage its exports, and pick up exactly where you left off.

EVERY EFFECT INCLUDED
One price, everything in. No watermark, no subscription, no in-app purchases, no account. Everything renders on your device — your clips never leave it.

Best with a fixed camera and a fast subject: birds and flocks, cyclists and runners, traffic and trains, waves and waterfalls, sports and skate lines, even stars.
